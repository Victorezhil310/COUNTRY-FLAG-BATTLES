Privacy Policy

Boost Flag Rush · the Country Flags 07 series · last updated 6 November 2026

The short version

Boost Flag Rush does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. The Android app requests no permissions at all — it has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. All 196 flag images and the game font are packaged inside the app.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in. The game starts immediately.
  • No analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking SDKs. There is no advertising in the app and no ad network is present in the binary.
  • No location, contacts, files, camera or microphone. None of these permissions are declared.
  • No scores leave the device. Score, combo and round progress exist only in memory during a session and are not written to disk or uploaded.

On-device features that touch data

  • Spoken commentary. The game uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone (for example Google TTS) to announce eliminations. The game hands plain text such as a country name to that engine on your device. It does not send text to our servers — we have no servers — and it is governed by the privacy policy of your own TTS provider.
  • Sound effects are generated locally from code, not downloaded.
  • Keyboard input. The “+FLAG” box lets you type a country name to add it to the arena. That text stays in memory on your device and is never transmitted.

Children

The game is general-audience content about countries and their flags. Because it collects no data of any kind, it collects none from children either. No advertising is shown.

Playing in a browser instead of the app

The web build on this site is the same game and likewise collects nothing. It does load the page itself from your hosting provider, which may record standard server logs (IP address, user agent, requested URL) under its own privacy policy. This is the only data flow involved in playing.

Third-party assets we credit

  • Flag images originate from flagcdn.com and are bundled into the app at build time; the running app never contacts that site.
  • The Montserrat typeface is used under the SIL Open Font License.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it and a new app build is published. We will not reduce your protections retroactively without notice here.
