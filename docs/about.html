About

Who makes Boost Flag Rush and the Country Flags 07 series.

Victor Ezhil

I build flag-arena games: a ring, every country in the world, and one moving gap that decides who survives. Boost Flag Rush is the latest of them and the first released as an Android app.

Everything here is made solo — the game logic, the arena physics, the emoji reactions, the spoken commentary, the launcher icon and this website. The project is open on GitHub so anyone can see exactly what runs on their phone.

Country Flags 07

The series name you see in the corner of the arena. Each episode is one arena: 196 flags enter, the gap eliminates them one by one with a reaction and a line of commentary, and one flag is crowned. Episode 07 is the build in this app.

What the game actually is

  • A single-file HTML5 canvas game, 9:16, wrapped in an Android WebView.
  • Fully offline: the app declares zero permissions and all 196 flags ship inside it.
  • Six difficulty levels from Easy to Chaos, plus a turbo BOOST and an infinite mode.
  • Commentary uses your phone’s own text-to-speech voice; nothing is recorded or uploaded.
